How the 4% rule works

The idea: if you withdraw about 4% of your portfolio each year, your money has historically lasted 25+ years. Turned around, you need 25 times your yearly expenses invested – because 1 ÷ 25 = 4%.

Happiness Number = monthly expenses × 12 × 25. Spend €3,000 a month and you need €900,000 – not a million. Spend less, and the number drops fast: every €100 you cut per month lowers your goal by €30,000.
